How ALLMA Works

Everything you need to know in 2 minutes

🧠

What is ALLMA?

ALLMA is your personal psychology coach available 24/7. Think of it as a conversation with a wise friend who really understands human psychology. You can talk about anything — stress, relationships, career, habits, or just how your day went.

👥

8 Specialists, One Coach

ALLMA has 8 areas of expertise. It's like having 8 specialists in one person:

🧠Auto — picks the best specialist for you
❤️Relationships — love, friendships, family
💼Career — work, burnout, goals
🏃Body — exercise, fitness, pain
🧘Mindfulness — stress, anxiety, calm
⚡Habits — building routines, breaking bad ones
🪞Shadow — deep feelings, past wounds
🥗Nutrition — food, diets, calorie tracking
💬

How to Use It

1
Just talk

Type whatever is on your mind. There are no wrong questions. Write like you'd talk to a friend.

2
Pick a specialist (optional)

Click one of the icons on the left side to focus on a specific topic. Or leave it on 🧠 Auto and ALLMA will figure it out.

3
Use your voice

Click the microphone button 🎤 to speak instead of typing. ALLMA understands all 8 languages.

4
Track your food

Tell ALLMA what you ate and it will count calories for you. Just say "I had pizza for lunch" and it tracks it. The calorie bar at the bottom shows your daily total.

🧩

ALLMA Remembers You

ALLMA remembers what you tell it between sessions. If you share that you're working on anxiety, or that you have a big meeting on Friday — it will remember and follow up. You don't have to repeat yourself every time.

🌍

8 Languages

Click the language buttons at the top to switch. ALLMA speaks English, Polish, Portuguese, Spanish, German, French, Italian, and Chinese. You can even switch languages mid-conversation.

🌙

Dark Mode

Click the moon/sun icon in the top right corner to switch between light and dark mode. Your preference is saved automatically.

🔒

Your Privacy

Your conversations are private and encrypted. ALLMA doesn't share your data with anyone. You can talk openly about anything without worry.

⚕️

Important

ALLMA is a coaching tool — not a replacement for therapy or medical advice. If you're in crisis or having thoughts of self-harm, please contact emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately. ALLMA will always provide local emergency numbers if it senses you're in danger.

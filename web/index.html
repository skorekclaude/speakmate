Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free. No credit card required, no trial period, no hidden limits. You get unlimited conversations with all 6 tutors, voice input, grammar corrections, and vocabulary tracking. We plan to add a premium tier later with advanced features, but the core experience will always be free.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a general assistant -- it won't correct your grammar unless you specifically ask. SpeakMate is purpose-built for language learning: every message you send gets automatic grammar corrections with explanations, new vocabulary is tracked and saved, and each tutor has a distinct personality that makes conversations feel natural and engaging.

What languages can I learn?

Currently, SpeakMate offers 5 English tutors with different specializations (general, Gen-Z slang, science, dating/social, art/philosophy) and 1 Brazilian Portuguese tutor. More languages are coming soon.

Do I need a microphone?

No. You can type your messages if you prefer. Voice input is optional but recommended -- speaking out loud is the fastest way to build confidence. Any device with a microphone works (phone, laptop, tablet).

Is my data private?

Your conversations are stored securely and used only to provide the service (tracking your vocabulary, showing your progress, maintaining conversation context). We don't sell your data or share it with third parties. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.
